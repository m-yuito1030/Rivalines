Privacy Policy

This explains what information Rivalines (the "Game") handles. Last updated: November 26, 2026.

This is an English translation of the Japanese Privacy Policy. If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.
Policy: we collect as little personal information as possible. We do not ask for an email address or your real name. There are no ads, analytics, or transfers to outside services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account name (chosen by the Player — please do not use your real name) and password. Passwords are stored in a form that cannot be reversed (a hash). Even the Operator cannot know your original password.
  • Company name, profile, and company color (optional).
  • Game data: routes, facilities, funds, and the results of your actions — whatever is produced by playing.
  • The last place you viewed: saved so you can continue from there the next time you log in.
  • Chat messages: the public chat keeps only the most recent 100 messages in the server's memory. They are not saved to files. They disappear when the server restarts, or when an account is deleted or suspended. Only when a message is reported do we store its text, as part of the report.
  • Report contents: only when you file a report, we store the reporting account, the reported account, and the text of the reason.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Email address, real name, home address, phone number, date of birth
  • Location data, device identifiers, advertising IDs
  • We do not use cookies. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking mechanisms.

3. How IP addresses are handled

  • To limit the number of login and sign-up attempts (a defense against brute-force attacks), the connecting IP address is held in the server's memory for about one minute. It is not saved to files or logs.
  • The access log of the Game's web server (nginx) is disabled.
  • However, depending on the carriers, hosting, and front-end services the Operator uses, information may be recorded outside the Operator's control.

4. Information stored in your browser

  • A login key (token) that keeps you logged in (valid for 14 days). It is stored in the browser's localStorage and is removed when you log out.
  • Display settings (showing station names, showing the minimap, etc.), and your language choice. These are not sent to the Operator's server.

5. Purposes of use

The information we collect is used only to provide and run the Game, to deal with misconduct, and to review reports.

6. Information that is public

Account names, company names, profiles, company colors, assets, and rankings are visible to other Players. Do not write anything you would not want to be public.

7. Providing information to third parties

Except where required by law, we do not provide the information we collect to third parties. The Game does not send data to outside services (such as advertising or analytics).

8. Retention and deletion

  • Information is kept until you delete your account.
  • With "Delete account" in the game, Players can delete their account and their company's in-game data at any time. Company names and profiles can be changed or removed in the game at any time.
  • Records of suspended accounts and of reports may be kept to deal with misconduct.
  • The Operator does not automatically delete inactive accounts.

9. Requests for disclosure, correction, or deletion

If the functions above are not enough, please contact us through GitHub Issues (a public place — do not post personal information or passwords). Just tell us the account name and we will check.

10. Changes

This policy may be changed as needed. A change takes effect when it is posted on this page.
