What is Rivalines?

Rivalines is a free multiplayer transit and city-building game that runs in your browser. Lay roads and railways, open bus, train and air routes, and watch towns grow around your stations — while other players do the same in the same world.

  • Build a network: roads, tracks, signals, stations, depots and airports, with timetables and routes you design.
  • Grow towns: good service brings passengers, new buildings and bigger cities.
  • Run a company: earn fares, build hotels, malls and stadiums, take loans, buy rivals and watch your share price.
  • Compete live: one shared world with rankings, auctions, partnerships and chat.
  • No install, no email: play on a PC or phone — sign up with just a name and password.

Available in English, Japanese, German and French. All names in the game are fictional.
